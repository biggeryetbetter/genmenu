A menu is represented as a series of sections, each with a series of items. The menu contains a place name, and each section has a title as well. 
There are two types of items: normal items and varying items. A normal item is just a name and price, so it can be used for any standalone item. A varying item can have variants and addons. Variants would capture aspects like size and spicyness, while addons would be things like boba, milk substitutes, and syrups. Sections and the whole menu are allowed to have addons as well, as sometimes addons are not confined to one item in particular.

See `schema.json` for the full specifications.
